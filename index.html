Walking down 14st

One of my favorite ways to start my night is by taking a nice walk down 14st. I'll typically take the train to 14st or surrounding stations and walk from 14st down to canal st. Sometimes I love to drink bubble tea or eat fries while I walk. But the main character of this walk is the music. As I spend most of the walk on the soundcloud backstreets listening to SZA's unreleased and demo music.
